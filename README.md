# Sales Performance Analysis and Interactive Dashboard Using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance and creating an interactive dashboard using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides insights into regional sales, employee performance, country-wise sales, monthly sales trends, and freight distribution across shipping companies.

Using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive year slicer, the dashboard presents business data in a visual and easy-to-understand format to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across different regions.
- Identify employees with the highest sales contributions.
- Compare sales performance across countries.
- Track monthly sales trends and fluctuations.
- Analyze freight distribution among shipping companies.
- Enable year-based filtering for comparative analysis.
- Present key business metrics through an interactive dashboard.

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### 1. Region-Wise Sales Analysis
A horizontal bar chart comparing sales across the Eastern, Northern, Southern, and Western regions.

**Business purpose:** Identify high-performing regions and compare regional sales contributions.

### 2. Employee-Wise Sales Analysis
A column chart showing sales generated by individual employees.

**Business purpose:** Compare employee performance and identify employees contributing the most to sales.

### 3. Freight Distribution Analysis
A pie chart illustrating the distribution of freight costs across shipping companies.

**Business purpose:** Understand the share of freight costs associated with different shipping providers.

### 4. Monthly Sales Analysis
A line chart displaying sales trends from January to December.

**Business purpose:** Identify monthly sales peaks, declines, and potential seasonal patterns.

### 5. Country-Wise Sales Analysis
A chart comparing sales performance across different countries.

**Business purpose:** Identify high-performing markets and explore opportunities for business growth.

### 6. Interactive Year Slicer
A year slicer allows users to select a year and filter the relevant dashboard reports.

**Business purpose:** Make it easier to explore sales performance across different years without manually filtering the source data.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Understanding:** Reviewed the sales dataset and identified the relevant fields for analysis.
2. **Data Preparation:** Checked data formats, missing values, duplicate records, and consistency.
3. **Sales Analysis:** Summarized sales by region, employee, country, and month.
4. **PivotTable Creation:** Organized sales data into meaningful summaries.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Created PivotCharts to visualize sales performance and freight distribution.
6. **Interactive Filtering:** Added a year slicer to enable year-based analysis.
7. **Business Insights:** Used visualizations to explore sales patterns and compare business performance.
